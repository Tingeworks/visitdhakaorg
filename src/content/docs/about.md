Visit Dhaka is a free, community-written guide to Dhaka, the capital of Bangladesh. It covers where to go, what to eat, where to stay, how to get around and what to know before you arrive, for first-time visitors and people who live here alike.

## Who runs it

The site is run by [Tingeworks](https://tingeworks.com), which looks after the site, reviews changes and decides what gets published. The pages are written and improved by a community of editors and contributors.

Visit Dhaka is independent. It is not run by or connected with the Government of Bangladesh, the Bangladesh Tourism Board, the Dhaka city corporations or any other official body.

## How pages are written

- **Well-known facts first.** Pages start from facts that are widely documented, and each one is then checked by an editor against an official or first-hand source.
- **Things that change are left to official sources.** Opening hours, entry fees, prices and visa rules change often. Instead of printing figures that could go out of date, pages point you to the official source, and we add details only once they have been confirmed.
- **Events are listed only with confirmed dates.** An event appears in What's On only once its dates come from the organiser or an official source.
- **No paid listings.** No one pays to be featured or linked on Visit Dhaka, and we don't show ads.

The site is new, and some pages haven't been through an editor's check yet. Always confirm anything important before you travel. See the [terms of use](/terms/).

## Reporting a mistake

If something is wrong or out of date:

- use the **Edit page** link at the bottom of the page to suggest a fix on GitHub, or
- email [team@tingeworks.com](mailto:team@tingeworks.com) with the page address and what needs changing

Editors review every correction. We fix confirmed mistakes as quickly as we can.

## Contributing

Anyone can help improve Visit Dhaka. Suggest changes with the Edit page link, or see the project on [GitHub](https://github.com/Tingeworks/visitdhakaorg). Contributions are shared under the site's [licence](/licence/), and the [terms of use](/terms/) explain what we can accept.
