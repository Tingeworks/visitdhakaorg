Fuchka is a much-loved street snack. Crisp hollow shells are filled with a spiced mix, often mashed potato and chickpeas, then dipped in tangy tamarind water and eaten at once.

## Where to try it

You will find fuchka stalls throughout the city, especially near universities, parks and shopping areas. It is a common outing in areas such as [Dhanmondi](/areas/dhanmondi/).

## Tips

- Choose a busy stall with fresh ingredients.
- Ask for less chilli if you are not used to spicy food.
- The water in the dip is a common cause of stomach upsets for visitors, so be cautious if you have a sensitive stomach. See [Health and safety](/practical-info/health-and-safety/).
