Kacchi biryani is one of Dhaka's best-known dishes. In "kacchi" (raw) biryani, marinated raw meat, usually mutton, is layered with partly cooked rice and spices and then cooked slowly together in a sealed pot. It is often served with borhani, a spiced yoghurt drink.

## Where to try it

Many restaurants across the city serve it, and Old Dhaka is closely associated with traditional versions. See [Old Dhaka](/areas/old-dhaka/).

## Tips

- It is a rich, filling meal, so you may not need a large order.
- Some places sell out early, so lunchtime is a safe time to go.
