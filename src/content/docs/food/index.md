Food is one of the best reasons to visit Dhaka. The city has everything from street stalls to restaurants serving rice, curries and grilled meat.

- [Kacchi biryani](/food/kacchi-biryani/): a celebrated rice and mutton dish
- [Fuchka](/food/fuchka/): the popular crispy street snack

## Eating well

- Busy places with a high turnover of customers are usually a safer choice for street food.
- Bottled water is the safest choice for drinking. See [Health and safety](/practical-info/health-and-safety/).
- Old Dhaka is known for traditional food, while [Gulshan](/areas/gulshan/) and [Banani](/areas/banani/) have many restaurants and cafes.
