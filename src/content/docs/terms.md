## How reliable the information is

We work hard to keep pages accurate, but Visit Dhaka is written and edited by a community and things in Dhaka change quickly. Information on the site:

- may be incomplete, out of date or wrong
- is general information for planning a trip, not professional advice
- has not always been checked by an editor yet

Opening hours, entry fees, prices, transport routes, event dates and rules can change without notice. **Always check with an official source before you rely on something**, especially for:

- **visas, entry and customs rules:** check with the official immigration authorities or a Bangladesh embassy or high commission
- **health and vaccinations:** speak to a doctor or travel health clinic
- **safety and security:** read your own government's travel advice
- **events, opening times and bookings:** check with the organiser, venue or business
