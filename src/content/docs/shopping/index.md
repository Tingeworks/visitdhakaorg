Dhaka offers everything from traditional markets to large shopping malls.

- [New Market](/shopping/new-market/): a long-established market area
- [Bashundhara City](/shopping/bashundhara-city/): a large shopping mall

## What to look for

- **Jamdani:** a fine handwoven textile from the Dhaka region. UNESCO recognised the traditional art of jamdani weaving as intangible cultural heritage.
- **Nakshi kantha:** embroidered quilts and textiles.
- **Shankhari Bazar** in [Old Dhaka](/areas/old-dhaka/) is traditionally known for conch shell bangles.

## Tips

Prices are often negotiable in markets but fixed in malls and shops.
