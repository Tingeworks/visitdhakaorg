Bashundhara City is a large multi-storey shopping mall in the Panthapath area, close to [Dhanmondi](/areas/dhanmondi/). It has many shops, restaurants and entertainment options, and it is a comfortable, air-conditioned alternative to the busy street markets.

## Tips

- Prices are fixed in most mall shops.
- Weekends and evenings can be very crowded.
