New Market is a large, long-established shopping area near [Dhanmondi](/areas/dhanmondi/). It was built in the 1950s and is known for its many small shops selling clothes, fabrics, shoes, books and household goods.

## Tips

- Bargaining is common in the market's smaller shops.
- Go earlier in the day to avoid the largest crowds.
- Keep your belongings secure.
