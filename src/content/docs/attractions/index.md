Most of Dhaka's best-known sights are historic buildings and river scenes. Many are in or near [Old Dhaka](/areas/old-dhaka/), so they can be combined in one day.

- [Lalbagh Fort](/attractions/lalbagh-fort/): a 17th-century Mughal fort complex
- [Ahsan Manzil](/attractions/ahsan-manzil/): the pink palace on the river, now a museum
- [Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban](/attractions/jatiya-sangsad-bhaban/): the National Parliament House, designed by Louis Kahn
- [Sadarghat](/attractions/sadarghat/): the busy river port on the Buriganga

Opening hours and entry fees change, so check them before you go.
