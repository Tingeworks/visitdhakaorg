Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban is the National Parliament House of Bangladesh. It was designed by the American architect Louis Kahn, and it was completed in 1982 after construction that began in 1961. It is widely regarded as one of the major buildings of 20th-century architecture.

## Visiting

The building is a working parliament, so access depends on the parliament's schedule and security rules. Check current visiting arrangements before you go.
