Ahsan Manzil is a pink palace on the bank of the Buriganga River. It was the residence of the Nawabs of Dhaka, and it was built between 1859 and 1872. Today it is a museum.

## What to see

- The domed building and its river-facing side
- Rooms furnished in the style of the Nawab era

## Visiting

It is close to [Sadarghat](/attractions/sadarghat/), so the two fit well into one visit. Confirm opening hours and ticket prices before you go.
