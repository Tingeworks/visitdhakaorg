Sadarghat is the main river terminal on the Buriganga River. Large passenger launches leave from here for towns across the south of Bangladesh, and small boats cross the river all day.

## What to see

The river front is at its liveliest in the early morning and in the evening, with launches, small boats and crowds of passengers and traders. You can take a short boat trip across the river.

## Tips

- Agree the price of a boat ride before you get in.
- Keep your belongings close, because the terminal is very crowded.
- Combine it with [Ahsan Manzil](/attractions/ahsan-manzil/) nearby.
