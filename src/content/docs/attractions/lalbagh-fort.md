Lalbagh Fort is a Mughal fort complex in Old Dhaka. Construction began in 1678 under Prince Muhammad Azam, and the fort was never completed.

## What is inside

- The tomb of Pari Bibi
- Lalbagh Mosque
- The Diwan-i-Aam, a hall now used as a museum
- Gardens and open lawns

## Visiting

It combines well with [Ahsan Manzil](/attractions/ahsan-manzil/) and the rest of [Old Dhaka](/areas/old-dhaka/). Confirm opening hours and ticket prices before you go, as they can change.
