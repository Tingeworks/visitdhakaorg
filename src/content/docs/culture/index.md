Dhaka's culture is shaped by Bengali language and literature, Islam and other faiths, and a strong tradition of music, poetry and art.

- [Pohela Boishakh](/culture/pohela-boishakh/): the Bengali New Year
- [Ekushey Book Fair](/culture/ekushey-book-fair/): the city's big literary event, held every February

## Eid

Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha are the biggest religious holidays. Around them, many residents travel to their home villages, so the city becomes much quieter and many shops and businesses close. Dates follow the Islamic lunar calendar, so they shift each year.

## Religious diversity

Dhaka has many mosques, and also Hindu temples, Buddhist temples and churches. Dress modestly and remove your shoes when you enter a place of worship.
