The Amar Ekushey Book Fair is held every February. It is named after 21 February 1952, the day remembered for the Language Movement, which is now observed as International Mother Language Day.

## What to expect

Publishers and booksellers from across the country set up stalls, authors launch new books, and there are readings and cultural events. Visitors and families come in large numbers, especially on weekends.

## Tips

- Check the dates and location for the current year before you go, as these are announced each year.
- Go on a weekday for smaller crowds.
