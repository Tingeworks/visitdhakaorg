Pohela Boishakh is the first day of the Bengali year, celebrated on 14 April. It is one of the biggest days in Dhaka's calendar.

## What happens

- Crowds gather in the morning, often at Ramna Park, for music and songs to welcome the new year.
- The Mangal Shobhajatra, a colourful procession with masks and large figures, is held in Dhaka. UNESCO has recognised it as intangible cultural heritage.
- People wear traditional clothes, red and white for many, and eat festive food.

## Tips

- Expect very large crowds and busy roads.
- Keep valuables secure and agree a meeting point if you are in a group.
