_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit visitdhaka.org, why, and what choices you have. We try to collect as little as possible. You don't need an account to use the site, and we never ask for your name, email address or any other details to read it.

## Who runs this site

Visit Dhaka is run by [Tingeworks](https://tingeworks.com). Tingeworks decides how information collected through this site is used.

For anything about your privacy or your data, email [privacy@tingeworks.com](mailto:privacy@tingeworks.com).

## Summary

- **Analytics:** we use Google Analytics only if you agree to it in the cookie banner. If you reject it or ignore the banner, it never loads.
- **Hosting:** the site is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles the technical information needed to deliver pages to you.
- **Fonts:** the site's fonts are served from this site, so your browser doesn't contact Google or anyone else for them.
- **Settings:** your accessibility and cookie choices are saved in your own browser, not sent to us.
- **Contributing:** if you suggest an edit through GitHub, your GitHub username and your changes become public.
- We don't sell your information, show ads or use your information for advertising.

## Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used, for example which pages people read, how they found the site and whether pages are working on phones. This helps us decide what to write and fix next.

Google Analytics is loaded through Google Tag Manager, and **neither loads until you click "Accept" in the cookie banner**. Until then, nothing is sent to Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager.

If you accept, Google Analytics collects information such as:

- the pages you view and how long you spend on them
- the site or search that brought you here
- your approximate location (country and city, worked out from your IP address)
- your device type, operating system, browser and screen size
- actions such as clicking a link or using the site search

Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. It gives your browser a random identifier, stored in cookies, so that repeat visits can be counted. It does not tell us who you are, and we don't try to identify anyone from it.

Google Analytics keeps this information for no longer than 14 months. Google processes it on our behalf. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

We don't use Google Analytics for advertising. The site tells Google that consent for advertising storage and ad personalisation is always refused.

### Changing your mind

Click **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page to change your choice at any time. If you reject analytics after accepting, the site turns Google Analytics off and deletes its cookies from your browser. We also ask again after 12 months.

You can also block Google Analytics everywhere with your browser's privacy settings, a content blocker or [Google's opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small files a site saves in your browser. Similar "local storage" lets a site remember settings. This site uses:

| Name | Type | What it's for | How long it lasts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `_ga` | Cookie (Google Analytics) | Tells visits apart, only if you accept analytics | 2 years |
| `_ga_<ID>` | Cookie (Google Analytics) | Keeps track of your current visit, only if you accept analytics | 2 years |
| `vd-consent` | Local storage | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics | Until you clear it; we ask again after 12 months |
| `vd-a11y` | Local storage | Remembers your accessibility settings, such as font size | Until you clear it |
| `starlight-theme` | Local storage | Remembers whether you turned on dark mode | Until you clear it |
| Sidebar state | Session storage | Remembers which sidebar sections are open while you browse | Until you close the tab |

Only the Google Analytics cookies need your consent. The rest are needed for the site to work the way you set it, and they never leave your browser.

## Hosting (Cloudflare)

The site is hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com). To deliver pages and protect the site from attacks and abuse, Cloudflare processes technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser and the page requested. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Fonts

The site's typeface, Google Sans, is stored on and served from visitdhaka.org along with the rest of the site. Loading it doesn't send any information to Google or any other font service.

## Search

The site search runs entirely in your browser. What you type isn't sent to us or anyone else.

## Contributing edits

Anyone can suggest a change to a page with the "Edit page" link, which opens the page's source on [GitHub](https://github.com/Tingeworks/visitdhakaorg). Editors also use the site's editor, which signs in with GitHub.

If you contribute, your GitHub username, the changes you made, any comments and the time you made them become part of the site's public history on GitHub. They stay there after your changes are published, because the history is how the site credits contributors and tracks where information came from. GitHub handles your account under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

If you email us, we use your email only to reply and keep it only as long as the conversation needs.

## Links to other sites

Pages link to official sources, venues and other sites. Once you leave visitdhaka.org, that site's own privacy policy applies.

## Where your information is processed

Google, Cloudflare and GitHub are based in the United States and run services around the world, so information may be processed outside the country you are in. Each of them applies its own legal safeguards to international transfers.

## Legal basis (for visitors in the EU and UK)

- **Analytics:** your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from Cookie settings.
- **Hosting and security:** our legitimate interest in delivering a working, secure site.
- **Contributions and emails:** our legitimate interest in running a community-edited guide and replying to you.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- ask what information we hold about you and get a copy
- ask us to correct or delete it
- object to or limit how we use it
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what happened before
- complain to your data protection authority

Analytics data is not linked to your name, so we usually can't find a specific person in it. Email [privacy@tingeworks.com](mailto:privacy@tingeworks.com) with any request and we'll reply within one month.

## Children

The site is a general travel guide and isn't aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the site uses your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change needs your consent, such as a new analytics or advertising tool, we'll ask again through the cookie banner.
