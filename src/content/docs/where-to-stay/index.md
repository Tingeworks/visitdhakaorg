Choosing the right neighborhood matters more than the hotel, because traffic makes it slow to move between areas.

## By area

- **[Gulshan](/areas/gulshan/) and [Banani](/areas/banani/):** the most popular areas for visitors, with international hotels, serviced apartments, restaurants and cafes.
- **[Dhanmondi](/areas/dhanmondi/):** a residential area with a lake, guesthouses and a central position.
- **[Uttara](/areas/uttara/):** convenient for the airport and the start of the metro line, but far from most sights.
- **[Old Dhaka](/areas/old-dhaka/):** the best base for history and food, but it has fewer places to stay, and staying there is a more intense experience.

## Tips

- Book ahead for busy periods such as around Eid and other holidays.
- Ask your hotel how long the trip to your main sights takes at different times of day.
- Check whether the price includes taxes and service charges.
