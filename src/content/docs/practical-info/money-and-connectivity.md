## Money

The currency is the Bangladeshi taka (BDT). Cash is widely used, especially for rickshaws, street food and small shops, so carry small notes. ATMs are common in the main areas, and larger hotels and restaurants often take cards. Check exchange rates and fees with your bank.

## Mobile phones and SIM cards

The main mobile operators include Grameenphone, Robi and Banglalink. SIM cards must be registered, so you will need your passport, and it is best to buy from an official outlet or the airport. Mobile data is useful for [ride-share apps](/getting-around/ride-share/) and maps.

Check current rules and offers with the operator, since these change.
