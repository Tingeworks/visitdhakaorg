Visa rules depend on your nationality and can change, so do not rely on a general guide for this.

## What to do

- Check the current requirements with the nearest Bangladesh embassy or high commission, or with your own government's travel advice.
- Confirm whether you need a visa in advance or whether one is available on arrival for your nationality.
- Make sure your passport is valid for the period the entry rules require.

Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport is Dhaka's international airport, near [Uttara](/areas/uttara/).
