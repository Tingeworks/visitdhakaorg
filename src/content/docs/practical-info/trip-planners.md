Other people's trip plans are a good place to start your own. These sites are run independently of this guide, so prices, ratings and opening times on them may be out of date. Check anything important with an official source before you rely on it.

## Trip planners

- [Shagotom](https://shagotom.vercel.app/): a scrapbook-style guide to the whole of Bangladesh, with ready-made routes, a trip list you can tick off, a map and a budget planner in dollars or Taka.

## Travel guides

- [Wikivoyage: Dhaka](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Dhaka): a free travel guide written by travellers, with sections on getting around, sights and where to stay.
- [Bangladesh Tourism Board](https://tourismboard.gov.bd/)
