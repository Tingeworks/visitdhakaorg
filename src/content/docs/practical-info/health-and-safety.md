This page gives general tips only. For medical and safety advice specific to your situation, use official sources.

## Before you go

- Ask a doctor or travel clinic about vaccinations and health precautions for Bangladesh.
- Check your own government's travel advice for current safety information.
- Get travel insurance that covers medical care.

## In Dhaka

- Drink bottled water and be careful with ice and raw foods if you have a sensitive stomach.
- Keep bags closed and phones out of sight in crowded markets, buses and streets.
- Traffic is heavy and pavements can be poor, so take care when crossing roads.
- In an emergency, call **999**.
