If you have an extra day, several places near Dhaka are worth a visit.

- [Sonargaon and Panam City](/day-trips/sonargaon-and-panam/): a historic capital and an old merchant town

## Others to consider

- **Savar:** home to the National Martyrs' Memorial, which honours those who died in the 1971 Liberation War.
- **A boat trip on the Buriganga:** you can arrange a short trip from [Sadarghat](/attractions/sadarghat/).

Traffic leaving Dhaka can be slow, so start early and check the route in advance.
