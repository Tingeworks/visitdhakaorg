Sonargaon was once a capital of medieval Bengal, and it lies roughly 30 km from Dhaka. Nearby, Panam City (Panam Nagar) is an old merchant town whose row of 19th-century houses is now largely abandoned and in decay. Together they make a popular day trip.

## What to see

- The old streets and buildings of Panam City
- The Sonargaon area, including the Bangladesh Folk Art and Crafts Museum

## Getting there

Go early, because traffic out of the city can be heavy and the journey can take longer than the distance suggests. Confirm the route and current opening times before you go. See [Getting around](/getting-around/).
