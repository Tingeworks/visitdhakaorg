Visit Dhaka is a community guide, and its content is free to share and reuse. This page explains the licences that apply and how to credit the site.

## Text: CC BY-SA 4.0

Unless a page says otherwise, the text on Visit Dhaka is available under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) (CC BY-SA 4.0). This is the same licence used by Wikipedia and Wikivoyage.

You can copy, share and adapt the text for any purpose, including commercial use, as long as you:

- **credit Visit Dhaka**, link to the page you used and link to the licence
- **say if you changed it**
- **share your version under the same licence** (CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible licence)

### How to credit us

A credit like this is enough:

> From "[page title]" on [Visit Dhaka](https://visitdhaka.org), CC BY-SA 4.0.

Online, link the page title to the original page and "CC BY-SA 4.0" to the licence.

## Code: MIT

The code that runs the site is available under the [MIT licence](https://opensource.org/license/mit). The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Tingeworks/visitdhakaorg).

## What the licences don't cover

- **Photos and other media:** images may have their own licence or credit, shown with the image. Where none is shown, ask us before reusing it.
- **Names and logos:** the Visit Dhaka name and logo and the Tingeworks name and logo aren't covered by these licences. Please don't use them in a way that suggests we endorse you or your work.
- **Material from others:** quotations, trademarks and content credited to someone else belong to their owners.

## Contributing

When you contribute to Visit Dhaka, whether through the "Edit page" link, a pull request on GitHub or the site's editor, you agree that:

- your text contributions are licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**
- your code contributions are licensed under the **MIT licence**
- you wrote the contribution yourself, or you have the right to share it under these licences
- any photos you add are your own, or are published under a licence that allows them to be used on the site, and you include the credit and licence the owner requires

The site's history on GitHub records who contributed what, and this is how contributors are credited under the licence.

Please don't copy text from guidebooks, other websites or official leaflets, even if you rewrite it slightly. Write in your own words and link to the source instead.
