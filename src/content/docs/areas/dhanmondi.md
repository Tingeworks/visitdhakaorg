Dhanmondi is a long-established residential area built around a lake. It has schools, galleries, shops, restaurants and cafes, and it is popular with residents for walks and evening outings.

## Good for

- Walking by Dhanmondi Lake
- Cafes, restaurants and street food such as [fuchka](/food/fuchka/)
- Being close to shopping at [New Market](/shopping/new-market/)

## Things to know

- Its central position makes it a practical base for getting to other parts of the city.
