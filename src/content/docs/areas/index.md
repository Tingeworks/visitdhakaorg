Dhaka is a big city and each area has a different character. Traffic is heavy, so it helps to group your plans by neighborhood rather than crossing the city several times a day.

| Area | Character |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Old Dhaka](/areas/old-dhaka/) | Historic, dense and lively, close to the river |
| [Gulshan](/areas/gulshan/) | Modern, upscale, many embassies and restaurants |
| [Banani](/areas/banani/) | Modern and commercial, next to Gulshan |
| [Dhanmondi](/areas/dhanmondi/) | Residential, with a lake and cultural venues |
| [Uttara](/areas/uttara/) | Planned northern suburb near the airport |
| [Mohammadpur](/areas/mohammadpur/) | Busy residential and market area in the west |
