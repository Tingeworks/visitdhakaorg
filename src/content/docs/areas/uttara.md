Uttara is a planned satellite town in the north of Dhaka, near Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport. It is quieter and more spacious than the older central areas.

## Good for

- A stay close to the airport, before an early flight or after a late arrival
- Access to [Metro Rail](/getting-around/metro-rail/), whose line starts here

## Things to know

- It is a long way from most sights, so it suits a short stopover better than a sightseeing base.
