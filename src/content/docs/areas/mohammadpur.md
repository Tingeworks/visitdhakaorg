Mohammadpur is a large residential and commercial area in west Dhaka. Fewer tourists come here, and that makes it a good place to see everyday local life, with busy markets, small restaurants and local shops.

## Good for

- Local food and markets away from the main tourist areas
- Getting a feel for how most Dhaka residents live

## Things to know

- It is mainly residential, so there are fewer hotels than in [Gulshan](/areas/gulshan/) or [Banani](/areas/banani/).
