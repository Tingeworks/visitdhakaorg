Old Dhaka (Puran Dhaka) is the historic core of the city, on the north bank of the Buriganga River. It is crowded, noisy and very rewarding, with narrow lanes, old houses, specialist bazaars and street food.

## What to see

- [Lalbagh Fort](/attractions/lalbagh-fort/), a Mughal-era fort
- [Ahsan Manzil](/attractions/ahsan-manzil/), the pink palace of the Nawabs of Dhaka
- [Sadarghat](/attractions/sadarghat/), the river port on the Buriganga
- The old markets and lanes around Shankhari Bazar, Chawkbazar and Armanitola
- Religious sites, including the Dhakeshwari National Temple

## Food

Old Dhaka is known for traditional dishes such as [kacchi biryani](/food/kacchi-biryani/) and Bakarkhani bread.

## Tips

- Go early in the day, before the streets fill up.
- Walking is often faster than a vehicle in the narrowest lanes.
- Keep your bag closed and in front of you in crowded markets.
