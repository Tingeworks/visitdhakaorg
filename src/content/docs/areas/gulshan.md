Gulshan is one of Dhaka's most modern neighborhoods and home to many embassies, offices, hotels, restaurants and cafes. It is one of the areas where many visitors choose to stay.

## Good for

- International hotels and serviced apartments (see [Where to stay](/where-to-stay/))
- A wide range of restaurants and cafes
- Being close to [Banani](/areas/banani/)

## Things to know

- It is a comfortable base, but the historic sights are on the other side of the city, so allow time for traffic.
- It is much calmer than Old Dhaka, and it gives a different picture of the city.
