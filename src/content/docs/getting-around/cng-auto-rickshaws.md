CNG auto-rickshaws are small three-wheeled vehicles that run on compressed natural gas. They are usually green, and they are quicker than a cycle rickshaw for longer trips. They typically carry up to three passengers.

## How to use one

- Agree the fare before you get in, or ask for the meter to be used.
- Some drivers refuse short trips or ask for extra, so be ready to negotiate or wait for another.
- They are open at the sides, so keep phones and bags away from the edge.

For a fixed price up front, consider a [ride-share app](/getting-around/ride-share/).
