Dhaka Metro Rail is an elevated railway that opened in 2022. Its first line, MRT Line 6, runs north-south from Uttara through central Dhaka, and it avoids the city's road traffic.

## Using it

- It is a good option for journeys along its route, especially at busy times.
- Station names, fares and opening hours are updated over time, so check the official Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited (DMTCL) information before you travel.

The line starts in [Uttara](/areas/uttara/), near the airport.
