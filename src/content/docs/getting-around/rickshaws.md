Cycle rickshaws are everywhere in Dhaka. They are often decorated with bright artwork, and they can get into small lanes that other vehicles cannot.

## How to use one

- Rickshaws do not have meters, so agree the fare **before** you start.
- It helps to know the name of a nearby landmark, since drivers may not know every street name.
- They are best for short distances. For longer trips a rickshaw will be slow.

## Tips

- Carry small notes so that you can pay the exact fare.
- Ask a hotel or a local for the usual fare on a route if you are not sure.
