Ride-share apps such as **Pathao** and **Uber** operate in Dhaka. They show the price before you book and give you the driver's details, which many visitors find easier than negotiating a fare.

## Tips

- You will usually need a local or international phone plan with data. See [Money and connectivity](/practical-info/money-and-connectivity/).
- Set your pickup point carefully, since streets can be hard to reach in dense areas.
- Motorbike rides are quick in traffic but carry more risk than a car.
- Check the app for the vehicle's number plate before getting in.
