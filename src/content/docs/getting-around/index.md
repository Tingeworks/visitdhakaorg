Dhaka has some of the heaviest traffic in the world, so allow far more time than the distance suggests. The best option depends on where you are going and at what time of day.

| Option | Best for |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Rickshaw](/getting-around/rickshaws/) | Short trips and narrow lanes |
| [CNG auto-rickshaw](/getting-around/cng-auto-rickshaws/) | Medium trips for up to three people |
| [Ride-share apps](/getting-around/ride-share/) | Clear pricing and a driver's details on your phone |
| [Metro Rail](/getting-around/metro-rail/) | Avoiding traffic on its north-south route |

## Traffic

Rush hours are long, and a short journey can take much longer than expected. Leave extra time for flights and appointments, and plan around one neighborhood at a time. See [Areas and neighborhoods](/areas/).
